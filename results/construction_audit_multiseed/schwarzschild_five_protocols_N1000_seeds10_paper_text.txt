Construction-sensitivity multi-seed rerun

For the Schwarzschild/Flamm benchmark, each diagnostic--construction--control protocol was repeated over 10 paired random seeds. The curved and matched-flat graphs shared the same radial and angular sample within each seed. For every run, C_log was radially binned using the common-bin comparison pipeline, and we computed Delta rho_r = rho_r(C_log)_BH - rho_r(C_log)_flat. The reported separation is |Delta rho_r|, summarized as mean +/- standard deviation across seeds.
