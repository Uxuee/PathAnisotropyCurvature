\section{Canonical data inventory}

The validated family calculation contains 80 fixed-protocol scan rows: two
families, four parameter values per family, and ten paired seeds. The anchor
audit contains 100 rows: two family anchors, five graph/control protocols, and
ten paired seeds. Every run uses $N=1000$, $M=1/2$, $k=16$, graph-shell radius
$r_g=3$, twelve common radial bins, and the calibrated radius factor 1.15.

All expected family, parameter, seed, and protocol combinations are present.
There are zero missing primary or secondary correlations. The maximum
radial-angular coordinate mismatch is $1.3322676295501878\times10^{-15}$ for
the family scans and $8.881784197001252\times10^{-16}$ for the anchor audits.
Complete vertex sets, connectivity metadata, valid-estimator fractions,
mutual-KNN subset checks, and exact or tie-explained edge matching are retained.

\section{Zero-parameter consistency}

Bardeen $g=0$ and Hayward $\ell=0$ reproduce the Schwarzschild/Flamm output
row by row for every seed. Coordinate hashes and common-profile hashes agree;
the maximum numeric difference is zero at tolerance $10^{-12}$.

\section{Repository and execution}

The repository is
\href{https://github.com/Uxuee/PathAnisotropyCurvature}{github.com/Uxuee/PathAnisotropyCurvature}.
Core estimator functions are under \texttt{code/}; resumable headless runners
and analysis scripts are under \texttt{scripts/}; validation outputs, summaries,
and figures are under \texttt{results/}; manuscript materials are under
\texttt{construction\_sensitivity\_latex\_project/} and
\texttt{submission\_jcn/}. Raw numerical results are retained in stable-header
CSV files, while completed family/parameter/seed units are stored as resumable
Wolfram MX checkpoints in the research repository but excluded from the journal
upload ZIP.

Calculations were executed headlessly with Wolfram Language via
\texttt{wolframscript}. The 100 Bardeen/Hayward checkpoint units required
27,701.3 seconds (7.69 hours), with individual units ranging from 116.5 to
1,099.9 seconds. The preceding Schwarzschild five-protocol audit required
3,636.84 seconds. Manuscript PDFs were compiled with Tectonic; Poppler was used
for page rendering and visual quality assurance.
